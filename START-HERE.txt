PCS WEBSITE — REVIEW AND UPLOAD PACKAGE
Star Atlas redesign for PCS 0.9.114
Prepared October 21, 2026

This package is the complete redesigned website: the new homepage, the four
guides and the Map demo, in the app's slate and sage with the wordmark's
four-point star. Guide wording is unchanged. It is not a new PCS application
build, and the live website has not been changed by creating this package.

REVIEW ON YOUR PC
1. Extract the entire ZIP to a folder. Do not open the pages from inside the ZIP.
2. Open index.html in your browser.
3. On the homepage, switch the model (Gemini, GPT, Llama, Local) in the app
   card: only the model name changes; the saved records stay put.
4. Try the Conversation, Library and Map tabs, click a screenshot to enlarge it,
   and pick a model in the "Start with ..." download line.
5. Open the guides from the top menu. Scroll one: the contents list on the left
   marks the section you are reading. Try the Map demo.
6. Make your browser narrow, or open the pages on a phone, to see the phone layout.

No installation or build is needed. Fonts, images and scripts are all included;
browsing makes no requests to other sites. External links (downloads, GitHub,
Discord and Ko-fi) need an internet connection. The bug-report form only opens a
pre-filled GitHub issue for you to review; nothing is posted automatically.

UPLOAD TO YOUR EXISTING WEBSITE
Repository: https://github.com/PCS-PersonalContinuitySystem/PCS-site

1. Open the repository's main branch and use Add file > Upload files.
2. Upload the EXTRACTED FILES AND FOLDERS into the repository root, where the
   current index.html is located. Keep the assets/ and screenshots/ folders
   intact, including assets/fonts/ and screenshots/crops/.
3. Replace matching files. Keep unrelated repository files and settings.
   Preserve .nojekyll; it is also included in this package.
4. Commit the uploaded changes to main. GitHub Pages will publish the update
   through the repository's existing setup.
5. Wait for the Pages deployment to finish, then reload the public website:
   https://pcs-personalcontinuitysystem.github.io/PCS-site/
   If a page still looks old, force-reload it (Ctrl+F5).

IMPORTANT: Do not upload just the ZIP. Do not upload an extra enclosing folder
around index.html. Upload the contents at the top level of this extracted package.

FILES THAT CAN STAY OR GO
The pages no longer load assets/site.css, assets/site.js or
assets/plain-english.css. Leaving them in the repository is harmless; you can
delete them later. The cyan and purple images in brand/ are a retired design
and are not used.

WHAT IS INCLUDED
- All six pages, their styles, script, self-hosted font (with its licence),
  the transparent wordmark, the app screenshots and their square crops.
- The Map demo's script, data and styles, unchanged.
- PRODUCT.md (product facts the site must keep) and DESIGN.md (the design system).
- README.md, this file, UPLOAD-INSTRUCTIONS.md, PUBLICATION-MANIFEST.json,
  WEBSITE-CHECKS.json and SHA256SUMS.txt for checking the extracted files.

Verification: each page was measured in a real browser at 1440, 768 and 390
pixels wide with no failures, and the homepage and guide controls were tested.
See WEBSITE-CHECKS.json for what was checked and its limits. Opening by
double-click is supported by the static file structure.
