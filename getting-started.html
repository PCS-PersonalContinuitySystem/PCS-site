Getting started / PCS 0.9.50 for Windows

Your first conversation.
One step at a time.

Start with typing. Add voice and other tools when you want them.

1. Open PCS→2. Your vault→3. Connect→4. Try it

Already using PCS? Go to updating and restoring ↓

01

Open the app

Download, extract, start.

Use the complete Windows ZIP. Extract it into a fresh writable folder, then open Start PCS.exe.

Python and ordinary dependencies are included. You do not need a separate Python installation.

Get the Windows download ↗
Keep the whole folder together.

Do not move just the launcher. GitHub’s automatic “Source code” archive is not the Windows app.

Check the download checksum ↗
02

Your saved continuity

Create your vault.

Choose Create a vault in the local page. Set a long, unique passphrase and keep it somewhere safe. PCS cannot recover a forgotten passphrase.

Saved conversationsRevisable memoriesNotebooks & plans

These records live in your encrypted vault. Original Materials and readable exports are separate.

Bringing an existing vault?

Restore your encrypted backup into the empty installation before creating a new vault. A fresh app folder does not automatically find your old data. Follow Updating and restoring below.

03

Choose your starting route

Connect one model.

Open Settings → Connections. Pick the route you want to use; the instructions below show one at a time.

Cloud · Uses your API key

Google

  1. Choose Google under Conversation connection.
  2. Enter your Google API key and keep the suggested compatible model to begin.
  3. Leave Use microphone off for your first typed conversation.

Provider API access and billing are separate from a consumer chat subscription. Permitted content is sent to Google.

Cloud · Uses your API key

OpenAI

  1. Choose OpenAI under Conversation connection.
  2. Enter your OpenAI API key and keep the suggested compatible model to begin.
  3. Leave Use microphone off for your first typed conversation.

A ChatGPT subscription does not itself provide API access or credits. Permitted content is sent to OpenAI.

On your PC · Compatible NVIDIA GPU required

Local

  1. Choose Local on this PC.
  2. Open Local model & files. For the guided route, choose Gemma 4 12B and its matching runtime.
  3. Use Download to folder… or Use existing files…, then wait for verification.

Keep model files outside the app folder. Typed Local needs no cloud AI key or speech pack. Model, hardware and voice details ↗

What about Qwen or a custom model?

Get one supported route working first. Custom GGUF setups need compatibility checks. Qwen is an experimental cloud voice route with regional setup and a separate Google/OpenAI memory helper; it does not support typed chat, retained research or saved Practice. Use the app’s Help for its setup.

After your first conversation

Add one thing when it helps.

Remember and inspect something

Enable saving and learning, then inspect the result in Continuity → Library. Open its sources and correct an interpretation if it misses your meaning. Use Saving & memory health to check pending work. Follow the memory workflow ↗

Use your microphone or a reply voice

Review the selected provider’s audio settings, enable Use microphone, Start, and allow browser access. For Local, select a listening pack and reply engine under Conversation → Local listening & voice. Local voice choices ↗

Read and keep a project notebook

In Documents, open the Materials folder and add a TXT, Markdown, selectable-text PDF or EPUB. Refresh the list while stopped. Read manually, or review the model and selected portion before preparing reading notes. Keep questions and next steps in a notebook.

Try tutoring or save Practice attempts

Proactive help starts Off. In Conversation settings, review its model and inputs to enable optional offers. For tutoring, check the problem and current step, then confirm them before asking for a concept, hint or practice.

For saved Practice, create a Tutoring & practice notebook with a lesson, enable Remember my practice, then use Practice → Choose lesson after Start. Submit answers through the Practice card; ordinary chat and proactive confirmation do not themselves save attempts.

Add a task or event

Open Calendar to create a local task, goal or event. Check its time zone and saved result. Choose Only when I ask if you want AI changes limited to direct Calendar requests. Calendar has no external sync or reminder delivery.

When you finish

Stop, Lock and Quit do different jobs.

Stop

Ends conversation and capture.

Lock

Closes access to the vault.

Quit PCS

Shuts down the host. Wait for “PCS closed.”

Closing the tab alone can leave PCS running. Check saving health before quitting. If saving needs recovery, keep PCS open and follow Recover saving with this vault’s passphrase.

For existing users

Updating and restoring.

Keep a verified encrypted backup and your previous app folder before moving to a new release.

Make the backup before you update
  1. Leave the vault unlocked, stop the conversation and let background work settle.
  2. Create an encrypted backup. Wait for the confirmed save and verify it.
  3. Copy original Materials separately; they are outside the vault.
  4. Use Quit PCS and wait for PCS closed.

A readable export is useful for reading; it is not a restorable encrypted vault backup.

Move into a fresh app folder
  1. Extract the complete new Windows ZIP into a new folder.
  2. Follow docs/RESTORE_VAULT.md to restore your encrypted backup before creating a new vault. Alternatively, copy the complete stopped data folder as the guide describes.
  3. Keep Local model and speech files in their existing separate folder, then select them again where needed.
  4. Open your records and check the restored results before removing any old copy.

Never run two installations against the same data directory. Native encrypted backup/restore supports vaults up to 2 GiB. New saved features may require a newer PCS reader; retain the pre-upgrade backup for rollback.

Open PCS Help, or docs/HELP.html for offline guidance. START_HERE.md is included in the download.
