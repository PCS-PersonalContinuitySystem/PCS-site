Getting started / PCS 0.9.114 for Windows

Set up PCS on Windows.

Download PCS, choose an AI connection, and send your first typed message. You can add voice and other tools later.

1. Open PCS→2. Your vault→3. Connect→4. Try it

Already using PCS? Go to updating and restoring ↓

01

Open the app

Download, extract, start.

Use the complete Windows ZIP. Extract it into a fresh writable folder, then open Start PCS.exe.

First launch prepares the bundled Python and ordinary dependencies offline. You do not need a separate Python installation. Optional AI models and speech packs are separate downloads.

Download PCS 0.9.114 for Windows ↗
Keep the whole folder together.

Do not move just the launcher. GitHub’s automatic “Source code” archive is not the Windows app.

Check the download checksum ↗
02

Your saved memories and notes

Create your vault with Quick Setup.

Use Quick Setup on the Conversation welcome screen. Create a long, unique passphrase of at least 15 characters and keep it somewhere safe. Choose Recommended continuity or Conversation only, then review what may be saved and shared with your chosen connection. Recommended continuity combines learning, correction upkeep, original-source recall and saved-topic awareness. Returning Quick Setup preserves your saved choices when you edit a connection. PCS cannot recover a forgotten passphrase.

Saved conversationsRevisable memoriesNotebooks & plans

These records live in your encrypted vault. Original Materials and readable exports are separate.

Bringing an existing vault?

Restore your encrypted backup into the empty installation before creating a new vault. A fresh app folder does not automatically find your old data. Follow Updating and restoring below.

03

Choose your starting route

Connect one model.

Choose Google, OpenAI, Meta or Local in Quick Setup, or open Settings → Connections for the full controls. For a cloud route, enter that provider’s API key and review whether to remember it in the encrypted vault. You can change supported connections later and keep the same vault.

Cloud · Uses your API key

Google

  1. Choose Google under Conversation connection.
  2. Enter your Google API key and keep the suggested compatible model to begin.
  3. Choose the Personal content or Public content only profile that fits your Google project’s terms. PCS cannot verify or change those terms.
  4. Leave Use microphone off for your first typed conversation.

Provider API access and billing are separate from a consumer chat subscription. Permitted content is sent to Google. This cloud route does not require a Local AI GPU.

Cloud · Uses your API key

OpenAI

  1. Choose OpenAI under Conversation connection.
  2. Enter your OpenAI API key and keep the suggested compatible model to begin.
  3. Leave Use microphone off for your first typed conversation.

A ChatGPT subscription does not itself provide API access or credits. Permitted content is sent to OpenAI. New OpenAI setups default to Balanced turn-taking; existing saved choices stay as they are. This cloud route does not require a Local AI GPU.

Cloud · Uses your API key

Meta

  1. Choose Meta under Conversation connection.
  2. Enter your Meta API key and keep the suggested compatible Standard model to begin.
  3. Leave Use microphone off for your first typed conversation.

Typed Meta needs no local language model, speech pack or Local AI GPU. Permitted text goes to Meta. Optional microphone input uses a local listening pack; Windows or Kokoro speaks replies. This is not native Meta speech-to-speech. Model access and API charges depend on your account.

Windows CUDA · Compatible NVIDIA GPU required

Local

  1. Choose Local on this PC.
  2. Open Local model & files. For the guided route, choose Gemma 4 12B and its matching runtime.
  3. Use Download to folder… or Use existing files…, then wait for verification.

The established Local route needs a compatible NVIDIA GPU. Model size, context, vision and GPU speech affect its memory requirements; no single verified memory minimum covers every setup. Keep model files outside the app folder. Typed Local needs no cloud AI key or speech pack. Model, hardware and voice details ↗

What about Qwen or a custom model?

Get one supported route working first. Custom GGUF setups need compatibility checks. Qwen is an experimental cloud voice route with regional setup and an explicitly selected Google, OpenAI or Meta memory helper; it does not support typed chat, retained research or saved Practice. Use the app’s Help for its setup.

04

Make the choices yours

Review your settings and send a message.

Finish Quick Setup after reviewing the connection, continuity choice and Calendar time zone. You can adjust the individual controls later in Memory & sharing and Save settings. New Personal content and Local setups use Only when I ask for Calendar assistance; Public content keeps it private and manual. Saving setup does not test a key or run a provider. Use microphone starts unchecked. Once the vault is unlocked and setup is saved, type a message and press Enter or Start & send ↗ to start and send it. You can also choose Start PCS first.

The introduction asks one question at a time and offers a recap before optional deeper questions. Skip, redirect or stop whenever you want. After your first conversation

Add voice, documents and other tools.

Return to a saved stopping point

With the vault unlocked, choose Load saved threads on the welcome screen to check saved threads, open Kept-for-later notes, local Materials and upcoming open Calendar plans. This is a local check, not an AI request. Choose a Calendar time zone to see upcoming plans.

Open a thread, reader or Calendar item to inspect it. Your most recently updated pinned open thought appears first, or a recent open kept thought when none is pinned. Expand its wording, sources and warnings, then choose Review & continue. PCS prepares an editable draft with your stopping point and next step, while protecting an existing unsent draft. Sending remains your choice. Load again when you want current results.

Use relevant memory and explore a connection

With personal-memory sharing enabled, Local 0.9.114 can check saved context for a question about you or an earlier project without a separate “use memory” command. The optional Help notice relevant memories in conversation setting is separate from that check; it is also included in new Quick Setup’s reviewed Recommended continuity choice. See the choices and connection differences.

On the Map, try Discover a connection and leave your own landmark beside useful evidence. A kept thought can Explore in Map with a temporary reminder of its title and next step. The separate Jev Library exploration trial requires review before sharing saved excerpts with TypeSafe.

Review a text attachment before using it

Attach or drop a UTF-8 .txt or .md file into Conversation. Review the complete text, then place it in an empty chat draft, open a Keep review, or choose Save reviewed text in Materials. Review alone does not send it to a provider.

The attachment limit is 480,000 bytes and 120,000 characters. Chat and Keep have smaller limits; an action that cannot fit leaves the attachment intact rather than shortening it. HTML and other document types belong in the ordinary Materials workflow below.

Saving in Materials requires PCS to be stopped and writes the exact text as an ordinary, unencrypted local file outside the vault. It needs a separate backup. An identical retry reuses the copy; another file is not overwritten. Web source drafts and previews remain separate.

Choose how PCS joins the conversation

Before Start, choose a Conversation pace: Follow my lead, Think with me or Take the lead. Wait for confirmation that your starting pace was saved; use Quiet or Stay here during a conversation to pause optional suggestions or keep the current direction. With Don’t save selected, the pace choice stays in this page.

Local also offers Allow brief quiet-moment openings in Local. It is off by default and is not restored from saved Settings. Explicitly choose it with Take the lead before Start. Openings require a focused, quiet Conversation page and stop when you type, leave, choose Quiet or Stay here, or Stop. The option can add a Local request before a reply. Read the limits and setup ↗

Keep wording for later, then choose where it belongs

Use Keep a stopping point below the composer to title and write where you stopped. Add an optional next step and pin the thought for your return. You can also use Keep wording for later on supported saved records and messages. Review the complete wording, title and recorded author. Keeping a quotation does not turn it into your own belief or automatically share it with a model.

The encrypted Kept for later shelf holds up to 128 items. Edit your own note, mark it resolved, reopen it or delete it. Saved quotations keep their wording and authorship; your next step stays separate. Continue places the complete, labeled wording in an empty conversation draft for review; it does not send it. Add quoted thought… can place a separate quotation in an existing notebook’s general notes or a lesson, or a new Calendar task, goal or event. Write your framing separately, inspect the preview, then save.

If the save result is uncertain, use Check unconfirmed addition before trying again. Removing a shelf item leaves an already-saved quotation in its destination; remove that quotation there. Quoted contributions remain separate from ordinary model-facing note text.

Remember and inspect something

Enable saving and learning, then inspect the result in Continuity → Library. Open its sources and correct an interpretation if it misses your meaning. Use Settled, links & lessons to mark an exact revision as your current view, connect how ideas developed, or keep a named lesson with its story and supporting memories. Library search includes separate My Map notes and Practice history scopes, with coverage limits shown. Use Saving & memory health to check pending work. Follow the memory workflow ↗

Use your microphone or a reply voice

Review the selected provider’s audio settings, enable Use microphone, Start, and allow browser access. For Local or Meta, select a listening pack and reply engine under Settings → Optional tools → Local listening & voice. Local voice choices ↗

Read and keep a project notebook

In Documents, open the Materials folder and add a supported PDF, EPUB, Office, OpenDocument, RTF, subtitle, HTML or common text/code file. Refresh while stopped and inspect its extraction coverage; scans need OCR elsewhere. Read manually, or review the provider and source scope before preparing notes. Prepare book works through an approved bounded sequence, saving a checkpoint after each portion. Resume preparation reviews the remaining scope. Local portions can use up to 80,000 source characters when the complete request fits. Search saved notes by topic and inspect coverage or extraction omissions. Keep questions and next steps in a notebook.

Read along, discuss, and return to your place

Choose a readable PDF or saved Web source, then Read along. An available local browser/OS voice speaks the extracted text and pauses at section boundaries. Discuss what we read prepares a recent passage and question for review before sending; Return to reading resumes the paused source.

Save bookmark keeps an encrypted position tied to that extraction. Materials → Documents → Resume reading also lets you review and remove old positions when the original changed or disappeared. Reading alone does not form memories or send the source to an AI.

Save a public source

Use Materials → Web sources, or Read / save source beside a research citation. Explicit capture supports readable public pages, PDF/EPUB, RSS/Atom and selected GitHub, MediaWiki and Stack Exchange responses. Review the available text and coverage before saving. Sign-ins and script-rendered pages remain outside static capture.

A saved source is a retained snapshot. Preview updated version keeps its reader and history link; review and save the new snapshot separately. Local AI does not turn on downloads automatically.

Choose activity memories or explore a question

Open Activity for manual details or optional automatic following. For spoiler-limited stories, review complete current memories before approving their use. Changed activity settings and saved accounts need fresh review; later progress changes apply to saved-memory context in a fresh conversation. Enabled public research remains available without extra activity approval or a restart, even while automatic following is waiting or pending. Story guidance uses known progress, but spoiler avoidance is best effort.

Explore another perspective uses a question and selected saved accounts to offer neutral questions. Review sharing, inspect the evidence and save only what you choose. Recognition and question relevance remain experimental. Activity and reflection guide ↗

Try tutoring or save Practice attempts

Proactive help starts Off. In Settings → Optional tools → Proactive help, review its model and inputs to enable optional offers. For tutoring, check the problem and current step, then confirm them before asking for a concept, hint or practice.

For saved Practice, create a Tutoring & practice notebook with a lesson, enable Remember my practice, then use Practice → Choose lesson after Start. Submit answers through the Practice card; ordinary chat and proactive confirmation do not themselves save attempts. A correction that finishes after you edit again preserves the later draft. Recovered correction text is offered for manual copying into its original attempt after you inspect the saved assessment; PCS does not replay the save.

Add a task or event

Open Calendar to create a local task, goal or event. Check its time zone and saved result. Choose Only when I ask if you want AI changes limited to direct Calendar requests. Calendar has no external sync or reminder delivery.

When you finish

Stop, Lock and Quit do different jobs.

Stop

Ends conversation and capture.

Lock

Closes access to the vault.

Quit PCS

Shuts down the host. Wait for “PCS closed.”

Closing the tab alone can leave PCS running. Check saving health before quitting. If saving needs recovery, keep PCS open and follow Recover saving with this vault’s passphrase.

For existing users

Updating and restoring.

Keep a verified encrypted backup and your previous app folder before moving to a new release.

Make the backup before you update
  1. Leave the vault unlocked, stop the conversation and let background work settle.
  2. Open Settings → Privacy & maintenance → Backup & restore. Choose Save current backup… for a copy using the vault passphrase, or Create protected backup… for a separate backup passphrase. Choose the destination in Save As and wait for the confirmed save.
  3. Copy original Materials separately; they are outside the vault.
  4. Use Quit PCS and wait for PCS closed.

A readable export is useful for reading; it is not a restorable encrypted vault backup. Prepared reading notes and retained Web sources are included in the vault backup; original local Materials files are not.

Optional automatic backups run while PCS is open, its browser page is connected, the vault is unlocked and the conversation is stopped. They pause during Don’t save. They retain the newest automatic copy from each of the 14 most recent days and 8 most recent weeks. Keep a separate copy if you need it beyond that window.

Move into a fresh app folder
  1. Extract the complete new Windows ZIP into a fresh folder and open Start PCS.exe. Do not create a vault there.
  2. Choose Restore an encrypted backup instead. Select the .pcs file and enter that backup’s passphrase, which may differ from the old working vault’s.
  3. Choose Check backup. Review the record counts and minimum PCS version, then choose Restore this backup. Restore never replaces or merges an existing vault.
  4. After confirmed restore, unlock with the backup’s passphrase. Copy unchanged original Materials into data/Materials, preserving filenames, contents and subfolders, then use Refresh list while stopped.
  5. Choose the automatic-backup destination again after restoring. Keep Local model and speech files in their existing separate folder and reselect them if needed. Inspect memories, notebooks, prepared notes, Calendar and Settings before relying on the restored copy. Keep the old installation and backup until these checks are complete.

Never run two installations against the same data directory. Native encrypted backup/restore supports vaults up to 2 GiB. Saved features can raise the minimum reader: larger Map preferences require 0.9.105; links, settled marks, lessons or queued suggestions require 0.9.106; whole-archive indexing and current Local context/cache settings require 0.9.107; saved proactive approvals require 0.9.110; preparation checkpoints with unextractable source ranges require 0.9.113. 0.9.114 adds no further reader requirement. Current Settings saves include the Local cache choice, including defaults. Opening, browsing or previewing alone does not activate these requirements. Check backup reports the actual minimum. See the compatibility guide and the included docs/VAULT_COMPATIBILITY.md; keep the pre-upgrade backup for rollback.

Existing proactive-help users should complete the updated Review model and inputs → Allow → Save settings review once. It explicitly includes selected Activity and automatic Activity following when enabled. Returning to an exact approved model, key and input combination can then restore that approval.

Change AI models and keep your saved records.

Your saved memories, corrections and notebooks stay in PCS when you change to another supported AI model or provider. For example, you can move from a cloud conversation to a compatible Local model without recreating your saved memories and notebooks.

  1. Save unfinished edits and choose Stop PCS. Wait for saving to settle.
  2. Open Settings → Connections and choose a supported conversation provider and model. Configure its API key or Local files as needed. Check the memory and Preparation helper choices separately.
  3. Review Memory & sharing for the selected connection, then Save settings.
  4. Start a new conversation using the same vault. PCS can recall selected saved evidence within your permissions and the model’s context limits.

Existing saved originals, memories, corrections and notebooks stay in the vault. A switch does not migrate the previous provider’s internal conversation state, and different models may respond or recall differently. Check feature compatibility before relying on voice, vision or tools.
