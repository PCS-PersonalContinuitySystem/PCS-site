Some thoughts become questions. Some become notes, or a small plan for tomorrow. PCS gives them connected places to grow—and gives you a say in what stays.

01Conversation

02Revisable memory

Keep context. Keep your say.

Inspect what was carried forward and where it came from. Correct a memory when it misses the point. Revisit it when your thinking changes.

Inspect the revision writer recorded by PCS separately from editable memory content. A copied provider name inside JSON is not proof of a cloud request.

03Materials and notebooks

Give your curiosity a home.

Keep original documents, topic notebooks and saved reference notes in one place. Read locally, prepare notes, and decide what should become conversation context later.

TXT, Markdown, unprotected EPUB and text-based PDF. Browsing stays local; prepared notes remain distinct from original files.

04Calendar and plans

Make a little room for doing.

Keep local tasks, goals and events alongside your ideas. Month and agenda views now sit inside a cleaner calendar that you can move through directly.

Ask directly to create or edit a plan, then inspect and adjust it yourself. No external calendar sync, bookings or reminders.

YOUR DATA, YOUR CHOICES

Personal context.
Personal control.

Your continuity belongs in a place you can inspect, revise and back up. PCS keeps its vault on your PC.

A local, encrypted vault

Saved continuity stays in your PCS vault. Keep your passphrase safe: forgotten passphrases cannot be recovered.

Sharing you can choose

Conversation and enabled sharing send selected content to your cloud provider. Local storage does not make cloud processing offline or end-to-end private.

Ways to take it with you

Create encrypted backups or readable exports. Original document files and readable exports are outside vault encryption.

A FEW GOOD QUESTIONS

A little clarity
before you begin.

Can PCS run offline?

Saved-record tools and Map v2 work locally without loading a model. The supported Local conversation route uses your PC without an API key or cloud fallback once its separate model and runtime are installed. Compatible Windows/NVIDIA hardware is required; Local vision and network research are unavailable. Google and OpenAI conversation routes need an internet connection.

Where is my memory stored?

Saved continuity lives in your local encrypted PCS vault. Cloud conversations and enabled sharing send selected content to your chosen provider, whose charges and data-handling terms apply. Original document files and readable exports are outside vault encryption.

Can I change what PCS remembers?

Yes. Inspect a saved memory, its linked sources and revision history, then use the correction controls. A requested memory can be created, an existing one revised, or a verbatim note saved; check which outcome actually completed. Pending is not saved. “Don’t save this conversation” controls PCS saving; it does not undo receipt by a cloud provider.

What do notebooks do?

Notebooks bring together a goal, your notes, linked original documents and prepared reading notes, with up to 24 linked sources. During eligible conversations, PCS can also save separately labeled suggestions. Linking a document does not mean PCS has read all of it.

Does Calendar connect to my other accounts?

No. PCS Calendar keeps local tasks, goals and events. You can make direct requests to create or edit entries, and inspect or change them yourself. It does not synchronize Google or Outlook calendars, invite attendees, book appointments or deliver reminders.

What does Local voice need?

English speech recognition is bundled. Spoken Local replies use an installed Microsoft David or Zira desktop voice, alongside the supported Local model and runtime. Listening pauses while PCS processes and speaks. Transcription can be wrong; check important requests in the transcript and saved outcome.

What does a line in Map v2 mean?

Both labels occur in the same supporting current saved records. You can inspect those records and source excerpts. Dates mean last saved or edited, not last discussed. A path follows displayed links, not a causal or psychological relationship. The map is a bounded local overview; it does not reconstruct old beliefs or use a cloud model.

Why can PCS be connected but temporarily not answer?

OpenAI may be in a rate-limit pause, Local may be processing or speaking, or a connection may be renewing. Read the status rather than repeatedly restarting. During an OpenAI pause, microphone and image forwarding are gated and typed drafts are preserved. When it ends, send deliberately; paused or rejected requests are not automatically replayed. Persistent failures need a Bug report.

Which model wrote a memory?

The memory inspector has a “Revision writer · recorded by PCS” section. That host-recorded provider/model is separate from editable JSON, which can contain a copied or incorrect authorship claim. A writer label does not certify that the transcript or interpretation is correct.

Where are the guides in the new download?

Start with START_HERE.md beside Start PCS.exe. Current guides are in docs, including HELP.html, which opens offline without starting PCS. Historical notes remain in docs/history and developer references in docs/developer. Keep package files together; the launcher checks its manifest. Use a fresh extraction and back up the whole stopped data directory, including Materials, when updating.

Does a ChatGPT subscription cover PCS API use?

No. PCS uses your own API configuration for cloud routes. ChatGPT subscriptions and OpenAI API billing are separate. Provider rate limits can also apply while credits remain. The PCS application is free; compatible Local use needs no API key.
