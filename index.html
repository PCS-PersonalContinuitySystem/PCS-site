02Revisable memory

Keep context. Keep your say.

Inspect what was carried forward and where it came from. Correct a memory when it misses the point. Revisit it when your thinking changes.

An unfinished question about orbits can be something to return to, with its source still in view.

03Materials and notebooks

Give your curiosity a home.

Bring a goal, your own notes, original documents and prepared reading notes together. Keep source material distinct from suggestions PCS may save during eligible conversations.

TXT, Markdown, unprotected EPUB and text-based PDF. Linking a book does not mean its entire contents were read.

04Calendar and plans

Make a little room for doing.

Keep local tasks, goals and events alongside your ideas. Ask directly to create or edit a plan, then inspect and adjust it yourself. Choose separately what Calendar can discuss and assist with.

No external calendar synchronization, invitations, bookings or reminders.

YOUR DATA, YOUR CHOICES

Personal context.
Personal control.

Your continuity belongs in a place you can inspect, revise and back up. PCS keeps its vault on your PC.

A local, encrypted vault

Saved continuity stays in your PCS vault. Keep your passphrase safe: forgotten passphrases cannot be recovered.

Sharing you can choose

Conversation and enabled sharing send selected content to your cloud provider. Local storage does not make cloud processing offline or end-to-end private.

Ways to take it with you

Create encrypted backups or readable exports. Original document files and readable exports are outside vault encryption.
