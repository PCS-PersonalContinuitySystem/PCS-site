02 Conversation

Start wherever your mind is.

Talk an idea through, ask another question, or take your time finding the words. Typed and spoken conversations have explicit session and sharing controls.

Choose Google, OpenAI or a supported Local model. With Local, Stop thinking cancels an unfinished reply and Skip speech stops current, queued or preparing speech while keeping the conversation open. Optional Kokoro voices offer another way to listen.

Actual PCS interface

The interface opens locally in your browser. Start a session to type or speak, with the Memory stream nearby.

03 Revisable memory

Keep context. Keep your say.

Inspect what was carried forward and where it came from. Correct a memory when it misses the point. Revisit it when your thinking changes.

Correct saved interpretations, compare revisions and inspect the recorded writer. Browse the Library, then use Continue this thought to bring a saved thread into a conversation.

04 Materials and notebooks

Give your curiosity a home.

Keep original documents, topic notebooks and saved reference notes in one place. Save supported public web sources for a quiz, study session or walkthrough, then return to the captured text in later conversations.

Keep TXT, Markdown, unprotected EPUB and text-based PDF documents beside notebooks and saved web sources. Paste permitted page text when a website cannot be captured. Original text, prepared notes and your annotations remain distinct, with draft and save-recovery controls for unfinished work.

05 Calendar and plans

Make a little room for doing.

Keep local tasks, goals and events alongside your ideas. Move through Month and Agenda views, select a date, and inspect its plans.

Choose whether PCS may assist with Calendar, then ask directly to create or edit a plan and inspect the result. Browse Month or Agenda views and keep time zones with events. Calendar remains local, with no account sync, invitations or reminders.

YOUR DATA, YOUR CHOICES

Personal context.
Personal control.

Your continuity belongs in a place you can inspect, revise and back up. PCS keeps its vault on your PC.

A local, encrypted vault

Saved continuity, retained notes and saved web snapshots stay in your PCS vault. Keep your passphrase safe: forgotten passphrases cannot be recovered.

Sharing you can choose

Cloud conversation and enabled sharing send selected content to configured providers. Local storage does not make cloud processing offline or end-to-end private. Requested web capture also contacts the source website.

Ways to take it with you

Create encrypted backups or readable exports. Saved web snapshots and bookmarks are included in vault backups. Original local document files need a separate copy; those files and readable exports are outside vault encryption.

Now in PCS 0.9.33

More resilient notebook and bookmark drafts, clearer save recovery, smoother Local cancellation and voice handling, and faster everyday work across Materials, Calendar and continuity. Optional NVIDIA listening and supported custom model choices give Local users more flexibility.

Before upgrading, keep a backup and read the update guidance. Some saved features require newer PCS readers even after they are cleared. Native Save As, automatic backup and restore support vaults up to 2 GiB; the compatibility browser download retains a 256 MiB limit.

Already using PCS? Update without leaving your saved information behind.

  1. 01

    Extract the portable ZIP

    Use a fresh, writable folder. Python and the application dependencies are included in the Windows package. Keep all files together.

  2. 02

    Run Start PCS.exe

    Let first-run setup prepare the bundled environment, then use the local browser interface. Create a vault or restore an existing backup and keep its passphrase safe.

  3. 03

    Configure your conversation route

    Use your own API configuration for cloud routes. For Local, download the supported model and runtime separately. Review sharing choices and begin with a short typed conversation.

Local AI and speech assets are optional downloads. The Windows ZIP includes ordinary Python and application dependencies for offline first-run setup; it does not bundle the conversation model, runtime, Whisper, Kokoro or OBS.

Recommended for Local AI: an NVIDIA GPU with at least 16 GB of VRAM. This is our recommendation for a smooth experience; needs vary with the model, context length, vision and GPU voice features. Google and OpenAI cloud routes do not require a 16 GB GPU.

Local hardware & voice details

Local conversation requires compatible Windows/NVIDIA hardware plus the selected model and matching runtime. Use Settings and Help for downloads and compatibility checks. Experimental Custom GGUF supports 8K, 16K or 32K context choices; compatibility is model- and hardware-dependent.

Use an installed Windows voice or an optional Kokoro CPU/NVIDIA voice pack. English microphone recognition uses a separate optional Whisper pack, with CPU listening by default and an optional NVIDIA route. Listening pauses while PCS processes and speaks; Skip speech cancels current, queued and preparing Local speech without muting later replies.

A Local recording ends at 30 seconds and processes the captured speech once. A warning appears around 22 seconds. A forced cutoff is partial speech, not confirmation of a complete instruction.

Read the setup guide before starting. A successful file check is not a guarantee that every hardware or background workload will fit.

PCS remains a pre-release. Offline automated checks are not a guarantee of live voice timing, successful capture from every website, or compatibility with every Windows device. Test with non-sensitive example information and keep backups.

A FEW GOOD QUESTIONS

A little clarity
before you begin.

What does a line in the Map mean?

Both labels appear in the same supporting saved records. Select a connection to inspect those records and source excerpts. Paths follow displayed links; dates describe current records’ last saves or edits. The Map can prompt reflection, but does not establish causation or discover hidden beliefs. Read the Map guide.

Can PCS run offline?

The Library and Map work locally without a model. Local conversation and memory can run offline after the required model/runtime downloads on compatible NVIDIA hardware. Optional Local web lookup contacts Brave and requested websites; separately approved preparation may use its displayed cloud provider. Google and OpenAI conversations need internet access.

Where is my memory stored—and can I change it?

Saved continuity lives in your encrypted PCS vault. Inspect linked sources, correct a memory and compare revisions. Cloud conversations and enabled sharing still send selected content to providers. Original local Materials files and readable exports are outside vault encryption. See memory controls.

What is new for Local conversation?

Stop thinking cancels an unfinished reply while keeping the session and your draft. Skip speech cancels current, queued or preparing speech; later replies can still speak. Local also offers optional NVIDIA listening, OBS vision, Brave lookup, Kokoro voices and experimental Custom GGUF profiles. See setup requirements.

Can I save reading material for later?

Yes. Materials brings together original documents, notebooks, prepared notes and saved public web sources. Capture one accessible HTTPS HTML, TXT or Markdown page, or paste text you are allowed to use without PCS contacting that website. Reading saved text is local; cloud discussion still follows sharing controls. A linked document does not mean PCS has read it all.

Does Calendar connect to my other accounts?

Calendar keeps local tasks, goals and events with Month and Agenda views. Enable assistance separately, then inspect the result of any request. It does not sync Google or Outlook calendars, send invitations, book appointments or deliver reminders.

What does “Don’t save this conversation” do?

It pauses new PCS saving for that conversation. Earlier records and settings remain. It does not stop a selected cloud provider receiving permitted conversation content or undo prior sharing. Review the data boundaries.

What should I know before updating?

Quit PCS and keep the earlier installation and a backup before extracting the new version into a fresh folder. Restore into an empty installation, with original Materials copied separately, or copy the entire fully stopped data folder. Never merge vaults or run two installations against one data folder. Native backup and restore support up to 2 GiB. Follow the update guide.

Is PCS free? Do I need a ChatGPT or Google AI subscription?

PCS is free and open source under GPL-3.0-only, with no PCS subscription or locked features. A ChatGPT or Google AI (Gemini) subscription is not required. For cloud use, connect your own OpenAI or Gemini API configuration; the provider’s API access, limits and charges apply. Eligible Google plans may include developer credits under their current terms. Local AI needs no cloud AI key. Supporting PCS is optional.

Where can I find help?

Start with START_HERE.md beside Start PCS.exe, or open docs/HELP.html offline. In-app Help includes searchable answers and Research and Practice guides. Join the PCS Discord community for questions and discussion, or report a bug on GitHub. Review exported reports before sharing; nothing is uploaded automatically.
