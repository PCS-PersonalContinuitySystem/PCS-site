How memory works

What you said.
What it remembered.
What you can change.

You choose whether PCS saves conversation text and whether it creates memories from it. These are separate settings.

An AI-written memory links back to the saved passage it came from. Read that passage, correct a misunderstanding, update a detail or forget a memory.

In a later conversation, PCS can search these records and provide relevant information to the model. Recall can miss details, and the AI can still get things wrong.

Choose your AI

Change the model.
Keep your saved history.

Your conversations, corrected memories and notebooks stay in your PCS vault—the encrypted storage on your PC. Choose another supported model and use the same saved records.

Models can answer differently and have different abilities. Switching keeps your saved records; it does not transfer a provider’s internal conversation state.

Storage and sharing

Know where your data goes.

Read the data and privacy guide ↗
  • Saved conversations, memories and notebooks are stored in an encrypted vault on your PC.
  • Cloud AI receives the content you allow it to use. Local storage does not make a cloud request private from that provider.
  • Original documents and readable exports sit outside vault encryption. Back up those files separately.
  • You can create an encrypted backup to restore your saved PCS data. Keep its passphrase safe.

Before you install

Common questions

Does my ChatGPT or Gemini subscription cover PCS?

PCS connects through provider APIs. Those use their own keys, access rules and billing. A consumer chat subscription does not by itself give PCS API access. A compatible local model does not need a cloud AI subscription.

Will it remember everything automatically?

No. Saving conversation text, creating memories and sharing saved information are separate settings. The model receives selected information, not your entire archive. Recall and AI-written memories can be incomplete or wrong; you can inspect and correct them.

Can I use PCS offline?

Local conversation and memory can work offline after the required downloads. Leave online services off and check the selected providers for other tasks, such as document preparation. You can also browse saved records, read documents, edit notebooks and use the manual Calendar without starting AI.

Can I use it on macOS or Linux?

The main download is for Windows x64. There is an experimental Ubuntu source preview with limited features. A macOS package is not included. Read the platform limits.

How do I close PCS?

Use Quit PCS and wait for “PCS closed.” Closing its browser tab can leave the app running. Stop ends the conversation and capture; Lock closes access to the vault.
