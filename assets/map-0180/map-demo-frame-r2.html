What to show

Saved labels are the topics and tags stored with each record. Wording adds words that repeat across records, and the names of people, places and projects in a Local account's statements; phrasing found in more than a fifth of records, such as “next step”, is set aside. Each current statement of a Local account counts as one observation; other records count once.

About this map
  • Themes are labels and repeated wording from your current saved records. No AI writes or groups them, and nothing is written back into memory.
  • A line means both themes appear in the same saved record. It does not mean they agree or that one caused the other.
  • Counts are records, not separate events. Several records can come from one conversation.
  • Dates are when a record was last saved or edited, not when you first had the thought.
  • Colors, constellations and positions are for layout only. They are not categories of you.

Dotted lines target the weakest 60% of displayed connections. Change this in Settings → Appearance → Map options.
