PCS website Map demo (assets/map-0180)

The files in app/ are first-party PCS 0.9.180 code, copied unmodified from
pcs/static/ in PCS-0.9.180-Source.zip:

  continuity_map_ui.js, map_layouts.js, map_constellations.js,
  map_fullscreen_ui.js, source_evidence_ui.js, style.css,
  continuity_map.css, map_space.css, map_constellations.css,
  map_shell.css, continuity_library.css, workspace_refresh.css,
